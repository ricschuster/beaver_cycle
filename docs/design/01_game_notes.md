# Beaver game: handwritten notes

Transcribed from handwritten notes (photo dated 2026-10-04). Wording is kept
close to the original. Items marked **(?)** were hard to read and need
confirming. Lines are grouped under headings for review; the original was one
flat bullet list.

## Life story

- The beaver starts as a baby beaver in the lodge and has a mama.
- The baby beaver has siblings and has to grow up and venture out of the lodge.
- The beaver has to leave the lodge and watch out for predators.
- The beaver has to make a lodge, find a mate, and start a family.

## Character

- A beaver can be male or female, and you can choose male or female.

## Needs and food

- The beaver has to chop down trees.
- The beaver has to eat lily pads and cattails, and inner bark.
- The beaver has to find stuck beavers, and the stuck beavers have to help the
  beaver. **(?)** (Unclear: do the stuck beavers help the player, or does the
  player help them?)

## Abilities

- The beaver can swim under water and can go really fast. **(?)**
- The beaver can hold its breath for 15 minutes under water.
- The beaver can slap its tail when worried, threatened, and/or scared.
- The beaver can put bum oil on its fur and comb its fur with its back feet.
- The beaver can grab mud with its front paws and put it on the dam.

## Building and territory

- The beaver makes scent mounds and dams.
