# Beaver game: questions

These questions help us fill in the parts of the game that the notes do not
cover yet. There are no wrong answers. Write short answers under each
question. If you are not sure, write "not sure" and we can come back to it.

## The big idea

- **Q1.** What is the goal of the game? How do you win?
- **Q2.** Can you lose? What happens if you do?
- **Q3.** How long should one game take? A few minutes, or a long time?
- **Q4.** Is the game for one player, or can friends play together?

## Being a baby beaver

- **Q5.** What does the baby beaver do while it lives in the lodge?
- **Q6.** What does mama beaver do for the baby? Does she feed it or keep it safe?
- **Q7.** How many siblings can you have? Can you play with them?
- **Q8.** How does the baby beaver know when it is time to leave the lodge?

## Leaving the lodge

- **Q9.** Which animals are the predators? (Wolves, bears, coyotes, owls, other
  animals?)
- **Q10.** How does the beaver stay safe from predators? Does it hide, swim away, or
    slap its tail?
- **Q11.** What does the beaver see when it first leaves the lodge? A river, a pond,
    a forest?
- **Q12.** Can the beaver get hurt? How do you get better?

## Your beaver

- **Q13.** When you pick male or female, does anything in the game change?
- **Q14.** Can you pick a name or a color for your beaver?
- **Q15.** Does your beaver get bigger as it grows up?

## Food and chopping

- **Q16.** Which trees can the beaver chop? Do some trees give more wood?
- **Q17.** What happens if the beaver does not eat? Does it get weak or slow?
- **Q18.** Where do the lily pads and cattails grow? Do they grow back?
- **Q19.** Does the beaver eat inner bark from the same trees it chops?

## The stuck beavers

- **Q20.** What does "stuck beavers" mean? How did they get stuck?
- **Q21.** How does the beaver find them?
- **Q22.** What happens when you find them? Do they help you, or do you help them?

## Swimming and abilities

- **Q23.** How does swimming under water work? Is there a breath meter?
- **Q24.** The notes say a beaver can hold its breath for 15 minutes. Should the game
    use the real time, or a shorter time so it is more fun?
- **Q25.** When does the beaver slap its tail? Does the player press a button, or does
    it happen on its own?
- **Q26.** What happens after a tail slap? Do other beavers hear it?
- **Q27.** How does the beaver use bum oil and the comb feet? Is it a mini game or a
    button?

## Building

- **Q28.** How does the beaver build a dam? What does it need?
- **Q29.** How does the beaver use mud on the dam?
- **Q30.** What do scent mounds do in the game? Do they keep other beavers away?
- **Q31.** How does the beaver build a lodge? Where is a good place for one?

## Mate and family

- **Q32.** How does the beaver find a mate?
- **Q33.** What happens when the beaver starts a family?
- **Q34.** Do the new baby beavers become part of the game? Can you play as one of
    them?

## Look and feel

- **Q35.** Should the game look like a cartoon or like real nature?
- **Q36.** What should it sound like? Water, birds, music?
- **Q37.** Is there a favorite part of the game that you can already picture? Tell us
    about it.
