# Beaver game: round 1 answers

Transcribed from the answered round 1 handout (photos dated 2026-10-04), then
corrected and extended by the author in conversation. Where the two differ, the
correction wins. This note resolves the two **(?)** lines in `01_game_notes.md`
about stuck beavers. Round 2 holds the follow-up questions.

## Answers

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Goal | Win by completing each task and getting every achievement. |
| 2 | Can you lose? | No. You cannot lose, and the beaver cannot die. |
| 3 | Game length | The handout says a long time and concentration (focus on game). Parked for now. |
| 4 | One player or friends | Friends can play together with different players. |
| 5 | Baby in the lodge | Eats, sleeps, and you can play in the dreams of the beaver. The dreams are played as side quests. |
| 6 | Mama | Feeds the baby, keeps it safe, and teaches the beaver. |
| 7 | Siblings | Up to four, and you can play with all of them. |
| 8 | Time to leave | When you are finished being a baby, mama tells you it is time to start your own family. |
| 9 | Predators | Wolves, bears, owls, fox, and lynx. No coyotes. |
| 10 | Staying safe | Hide, swim, or slap the tail, as a warning or to fight. |
| 11 | First sight outside | A pond with a forest around it, connected to a river. Lily pads. |
| 12 | Hurt | The beaver can be hurt by predators and gets better by eating lily pads. |
| 20 | Stuck beavers | Beavers stuck in snow or swarmed by predators. They are not the player. They are potential guides. |
| 21 | Finding them | The player travels around, and spots and hears a stuck beaver. |
| 22 | When found | You rescue it, and then it is your guide. |

## Decisions from the corrections

- **Game length** is parked until round 2 and the area editor give a better
  feel for it.
- **No coyotes.** They are not native to the Almtal anyway.
- **Seasons: yes.** Snow is part of the game, and stuck-in-snow is literal.
- **Growing up takes time.** A baby takes one year to become a teenager. The
  teenager stays one more year with the family and helps out around the pond.
- **The family has a papa** as well as a mama.

## Effect on the location

- A pond, forest, and river fit the south end of the Almsee with the Aagbach
  flowing in and the Alm flowing out.
- Seasons and snow mean the valley floor may not be the whole picture. Winter
  changes the pond, the food, and where beavers can travel.
- Of the predators, only the lynx is confirmed for the area from sources found
  so far. See `03_location.md`.
