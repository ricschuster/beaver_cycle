## What the answers settle

- **Goal loop:** get a mate, have kits, find the guides, and collect
  achievements. After everything is done the game keeps going, and each baby
  starts its own achievement journey.
- **Generations:** you can play as a baby, and it starts all over again (Q34,
  Q37, Q40). This is the author's favorite part.
- **Beaver:** male and female play the same except for giving birth. Color
  must be a natural beaver color. The beaver grows bigger with age.
- **Food:** not eating has no penalty. Bark and inner bark are eaten, and
  some trees give more wood. Lily pads and cattails grow back near water and
  die in winter, so the family stores food ahead of time.
- **Seasons:** they change slowly, and the player has to prepare (store food
  and lily pads) before winter.
- **Time:** the baby year can be played in full or skipped.
- **Controls:** tail slap, bum oil, and comb feet are each a button. Other
  beavers hear a tail slap.
- **Building:** dams need wood, mud, and rocks. The lodge sits in the middle
  of the pond and uses the same materials. Mud comes from the pond bottom and
  the side. Scent mounds mark a territory as taken.
- **Family:** teenagers repair the lodge, dams, and food cache. Papa teaches
  repairs. Mama and papa tell the grown teenager it is time to leave.
- **Guides:** a guide gives directions. Rescuing means digging through snow or
  fighting predators off. Stuck beavers come from other families.
- **Style and sound:** stylized 3D, a toned-down cartoon look, with rushing
  water, animal sounds, and music. Multiplayer is confirmed.
- **Dreams:** they happen while the beaver sleeps. One example is candy land.
