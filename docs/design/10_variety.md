# Variety in a game that never ends

The game has no end and repeats with each baby (Q40, `09_game_length_and_speed.md`).
This note holds what keeps it interesting. It is a first draft from one
conversation and the questions below are open.

## What the author said

- Things degrade over time and need repair.
- Families change.
- NPC beavers can be part of that.

## How this fits the answers so far

- **Repair already has a place.** Teenagers repair the lodge, dams, and food
  cache (Q48), and papa shows how to repair the lodge (Q49). Decay gives
  those jobs a reason to exist all year.
- **Seasons give pressure.** Ten days per season, food to store before winter
  (Q44, Q45), slow change (Q46).
- **Generations give change.** Each baby starts its own achievements (Q40).
  Families change as babies grow up, teenagers leave (Q50), and a new family
  starts.
- **Other beavers already exist.** Stuck beavers come from other families
  (Q53) and become guides (Q51).

## Open questions

- What wears out, and how fast: dam leaks, lodge sticks, food cache, scent
  mounds? Does the player see damage coming?
- What can go wrong that is not a loss? The beaver cannot die or lose
  (round 1 Q2), so damage has to be a chore or a small setback, not a failure.
- How does a family change besides growing up: new neighbors, a visiting
  family, a mate who arrives, a teenager who moves away to a place the player
  can visit?
- What do NPC beavers do: live in other lodges, repair their own, trade or
  help, need rescue, come back later as guides?
- Does the world change between generations, for example a dam that was built
  earlier is still there and ages?
