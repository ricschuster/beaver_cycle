# Variety in a game that never ends

The game has no end and repeats with each baby (Q40, `09_game_length_and_speed.md`).
This note holds what keeps it interesting. It started as a draft from one
conversation and now holds the round 3 rules. The questions below are open.

## What the author said

- Things degrade over time and need repair.
- Families change.
- NPC beavers can be part of that.

## How this fits the answers so far

- **Repair already has a place.** Teenagers repair the lodge, dams, and food
  cache (Q48), and papa shows how to repair the lodge (Q49). Decay gives
  those jobs a reason to exist all year.
- **Seasons give pressure.** Ten days per season, food to store before winter
  (Q44, Q45), slow change (Q46).
- **Generations give change.** Each baby starts its own achievements (Q40).
  Families change as babies grow up, teenagers leave (Q50), and a new family
  starts.
- **Other beavers already exist.** Stuck beavers come from other families
  (Q53) and become guides (Q51).

## Round 3 rules

The author answered the variety questions in round 3
(`12_round3_answers.md`). These are the rules that follow.

### Things wear out

- The dam wears out first, then the food store (Q54). The lodge is not named,
  so it wears slowest until the author says otherwise.
- A worn out dam looks broken (Q54).
- The player sees wear. A circle glows around the dam, lodge, or food store that
  needs repair. No beaver announces it (Q55).
- Ignoring it has one cost: the thing breaks and has to be rebuilt (Q56). That
  is a chore, not a loss, which fits the no losing rule.
- The beavers repair the broken thing, or build it again (Q57). Repair is
  building again with the same carry and place action, so there is no repair
  button (`15_build_and_repair.md`).

### Families change

- When the beaver grows up, the family has more kits (Q58).
- Baby beavers differ by color, within beaver colors such as brown (Q59, Q14).
- A teenager who left can be met again. The parents move between parts of the
  pond, so where you find the family changes (Q60, Q61).

### Other beavers

- Other beavers move about the pond and change where they live (Q61).
- The player can help, trade, and play with other beavers (Q62).
- A visitor notices the scent mounds, and then a new beaver lives nearby (Q62,
  Q30). Scent mounds are how neighbors arrive.

### A new generation

- The dam and lodge from before still stand and get old (Q63). Decay carries
  across generations, so a new baby inherits buildings that need repair.
- Each generation starts in a different pond (Q64).

### Sleeping and speed

- Sleep is either played as the beaver's dream, or skipped to the next day
  (Q65). The author gave both, so which one is open.
- The speed-up button is press and hold. The beaver never gets tired (Q66).

## Open questions

- How fast does each thing wear out? `15_build_and_repair.md` has starting
  values to tune on a tablet. The lodge is slowest, which the author has not
  stated.
- The open build questions are in `15_build_and_repair.md`: dam placement,
  pond water, rocks, and moving the lodge.
- Does the dream at night replace the skip, or does the player choose each
  night (Q65)?
- Q64 says each generation starts in a different pond. How does the new baby
  reach it, and how does that fit the old dam and lodge that still stand (Q63)?
- What does help, trade, and play with other beavers look like in practice
  (Q62)?
