# Beaver game: questions, round 3

These questions are about what keeps the game fun over a long time. There are
no wrong answers. Write short answers under each question. If you are not
sure, write "not sure" and we can come back to it. Your earlier answers say
that things wear out and need repair, that families change, and that other
beavers live in the valley. These questions follow up on that.

## Things wear out

- **Q54.** What wears out first: the dam, the lodge, or the food store? What
    does it look like when it is worn out?
- **Q55.** How does the player know something needs repair? Does a beaver tell
    you, or do you see it?
- **Q56.** What happens if you ignore a broken dam? Remember the beaver cannot
    lose, so think of something that is annoying but not scary.
- **Q57.** How do you repair something? What does the beaver need, and is it a
    button?

## Families change

- **Q58.** How does a family change besides babies growing up? Think about new
    neighbors, visitors, or a mate who arrives.
- **Q59.** Are the baby beavers all different? What makes one baby different
    from another?
- **Q60.** After a teenager leaves to start its own family, can you meet it
    again? Where?

## Other beavers

- **Q61.** What do the other beavers do all day?
- **Q62.** Can the player help other beavers, trade with them, or play with
    them? What happens when a visitor comes to your pond?

## A new generation

- **Q63.** When you play the next baby, should the dam and lodge from before
    still be there and get old? Why or why not?
- **Q64.** What is one thing that makes a new generation different from the one
    before?

## Sleeping and speed

- **Q65.** You can sleep through the night. What do you see or do while the
    beaver sleeps?
- **Q66.** Later the player may get a speed-up button. How should it work? Do
    you hold it, press it once, or does the beaver get tired?
