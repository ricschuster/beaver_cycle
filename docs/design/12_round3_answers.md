# Beaver game: round 3 answers

Transcribed from the answered round 3 handout (three photos dated 2026-10-04,
Q54 to Q66). Spelling is tidied and the meaning is kept. Doubtful readings were
confirmed by the author on 2026-10-04. The photos stay the source.

Round 2 is in `07_round2_answers.md`. The questions are in
`11_game_questions_round3.md`.

## Answers

### Things wear out

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 54 | What wears out first | First the dam, then the food store. A worn out dam looks broken. |
| 55 | How you know it needs repair | A circle glows around the dam, lodge, or food store. The player sees it, no beaver tells you. |
| 56 | Ignoring a broken dam | If the dam, lodge, or food store is ignored, it breaks and you have to rebuild it. |
| 57 | How to repair | The beavers repair the broken thing, or build it again. Whether it is a button is not sure yet. Decide it together with how things are built. |

### Families change

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 58 | How a family changes | When the beaver grows up, the family has other kits. |
| 59 | Babies all different | Yes, all the baby beavers are different, by color, as the author confirmed. |
| 60 | Meeting a teenager again | Yes, but the mama and papa have switched places, so they will be in a different part of the pond. |

### Other beavers

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 61 | What other beavers do all day | The mama and papa moved to a different part of the pond. |
| 62 | Help, trade, play, visitors | Yes, the player can help, trade, and play with other beavers. When a visitor comes, they notice the scent mounds, and then there is a new beaver living near. |

### A new generation

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 63 | Old dam and lodge stay | Yes, the dam and lodge from before still stand and get old, as the author confirmed. |
| 64 | What is different | That year, in a different pond, to start in. |

### Sleeping and speed

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 65 | While the beaver sleeps | Either you go into the beaver's dream, or you just go to the next day. |
| 66 | Speed-up button | Press and hold it. The beaver can never get tired. |
