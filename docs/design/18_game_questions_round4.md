# Beaver game: questions, round 4

These questions are about dams and ponds. There are no wrong answers. Write
short answers under each question. If you are not sure, write "not sure" and we
can come back to it. You said that a dam needs wood, mud, and rocks, and that
the lodge sits in the middle of the pond. We now have to decide what a dam does
in the game and where you can build it.

## What a dam does

- **Q67.** When your beaver finishes a dam, does water pile up behind it and
    make a pond? Can you see the pond and swim in it?
- **Q68.** Do you need a dam before you have a lodge? Does the beaver build the
    dam first, and then the lodge in the pond?
- **Q69.** What is the pond good for? Fish? Lily pads? A safe place to hide?
    Something else?
- **Q70.** Can you build more than one dam? Could your family have two or three
    ponds?
- **Q71.** What happens when a dam is broken? My idea is that the pond slowly
    gets smaller and the water runs away down the stream. It is annoying but not
    scary, and you can fix it. Is that right?

## Where and how big

- **Q72.** Where can you build a dam? Only in special places that the game
    marks, or anywhere along a stream?
- **Q73.** How big is the first dam? Should the first dam be small, with bigger
    ones coming later?
- **Q74.** How many places to build should there be? Should there be only a few
    good places in each part of the valley, so you have to pick one?

## Wood, mud, and rocks

- **Q75.** Does a bigger dam need more stuff? Does it need more wood, mud, and
    rocks than a small one?
- **Q76.** Are there rocks everywhere in the valley? Or only near some banks of
    the lake and streams?
