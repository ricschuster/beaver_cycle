## What this means for the design

- A dam makes a real pond you can see and swim in. A broken dam lets it shrink
  back to the stream, and the player repairs it (Q71, Q54 to Q57).
- The order is dam first, quickly, then the lodge in the pond (Q68). The first
  dam is small and later dams are bigger (Q73), and a bigger dam costs more wood,
  mud, and rocks (Q75).
- Ponds are a safe place from predators and where lily pads grow (Q69).
- Dam spots are marked by the game, at places where a stream meets a pond or
  lake (Q72). Not anywhere along a stream. This fits the dam site scan.
- Rocks are everywhere, so rocks need no map data (Q76).

## Open points

- Q67, second line: probably "you can swim in the other place too". It may mean
  you can swim in the stream as well. Ask.
- Q70: "more than three ponds" while Q74 says three places. Is three the number
  of dam spots per part of the valley, or a limit? Ask.
- Q72 and Q74: does a dam spot only exist where a stream already meets water, or
  does the new pond itself create the next spot? Ask.
