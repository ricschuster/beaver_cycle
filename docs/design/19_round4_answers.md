## What this means for the design

- A dam makes a real pond you can see and swim in. A broken dam lets it shrink
  back to the stream, and the player repairs it (Q71, Q54 to Q57).
- The order is dam first, quickly, then the lodge in the pond (Q68). The first
  dam is small and later dams are bigger (Q73), and a bigger dam costs more wood,
  mud, and rocks (Q75).
- Ponds are a safe place from predators and where lily pads grow (Q69).
- Dam spots are marked by the game, at places where a stream meets a pond or
  lake (Q72). Not anywhere along a stream. This fits the dam site scan. There is
  no limit on dams or ponds (Q70), and a new pond can open the next spot (Q74),
  so spots are found as the ponds grow, not fixed up front.
- Rocks are everywhere where it makes sense (Q76), so they need no map data, only a rule such as near banks and streams.
