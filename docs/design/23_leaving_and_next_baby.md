`22_life_stages.md`, which has the stages, Mama and Papa, and the coats.

## What is open

How the player gets from the old life to the next baby is not answered. Three
ways, with a recommendation.

1. **The player keeps the grown beaver and the next baby is born to it.** The
   adult builds a new lodge and finds a mate, and then plays the baby. The
   baby's pond is the new one. The old lodge, with Mama and Papa, is the
   previous generation.
2. **The player becomes the baby of the grown beaver at once** (the grown
   beaver stays in the world as Mama or Papa). The simplest, and it matches
   "play the next baby" best. The new lodge is the grown beaver's work, so the
   adult years are the only time the player builds.
3. **A cut scene or a fade** from the adult to a new baby in another pond, with
   no new lodge from the player.

Recommendation: 1, in small steps, so the adult years are real play (the new
lodge, the dam, the mate) and the baby comes as a reward. It needs a mate, so
the first step is smaller than it looks.

## Proposed steps

1. **Mama says go (built).** When the teenager year ends, a toast says it is time
   to start a family of one's own, and Mama and Papa are no longer drawn. The
   player beaver is an adult. Nothing more happens yet.
2. **A different pond.** Pick the new pond from the play area: the nearest
   valid lodge spot (`findSite`) at least a set distance from the old lodge, or
   a dam spot's pond. The player swims there on its own. This needs the author's
   choice of distance and whether a tap on a map helps.
3. **A mate.** A visitor beaver who comes to the new lodge (Q62), after the lodge
   is whole. A first form: a beaver appears at the water hole on a later day.
4. **Kits.** When the mate has lived in the new lodge for a season, the family
   has a baby (Q58), and `newLife` starts for the baby, born in the new lodge on
   its nest (the same start the baby has now).
