# Beaver game: questions, round 5

These questions come from your two pages "beaver game fix up #1 and #2". Thank you, they are great ideas. There are no wrong
answers. Write short answers under each question. If you are not sure, write "not sure" and we can come back to it. The
pages you crossed out (more rocks in the pond, rocks that come back) are left out.

## Mending a dam

- **Q77.** You wrote that the dam disappears when you try to mend it. What did you see? Did the whole dam go away, or did
    it only look broken and then start new? Tell me what you pressed and what happened.
- **Q78.** When a dam is broken, should mending it cost less than building a new one? For example half the wood, mud, and
    rocks? Should it keep its size, so a big dam stays a big dam?

## The lodge when the water is low

- **Q79.** The door of the lodge is under water, so the beaver cannot swim in when the pond is low. How should it get in?
    Walk in over the mud? Or should the pond never get that low?

## The dream: candy land

- **Q80.** Cotton candy boats: how do you ride one? Do you steer it with the stick like swimming, or does it float along
    the chocolate river by itself? How do you get out?
- **Q81.** When you build a dam and a lodge in the dream, do they stay for the next dream, or is everything new every
    night, as the land is now?
- **Q82.** When you chop down a candy tree, what do you get? Candy logs to build with? Something to eat?
- **Q83.** Other ponds to play in: how many? What is in them? Lily pads? Marshmallow beavers? Something new?
- **Q84.** The golden beaver statue: is it hard to find, or is it always somewhere in the land? How long should the speed
    boost last, a few seconds or a long time?

## The dream: veggie garden

- **Q85.** Can you choose the veggie garden dream from the first night, or is it a prize you earn? For example after you
    have had candy land five times.
- **Q86.** Which vegetables are the beavers? Carrot, pea, tomato, radish, pumpkin? Name the ones you want, and their
    names, like Puff in candy land.
- **Q87.** What does your beaver look like, a mix of different vegetables? Which parts are which? (Nose, tail, body, feet.)
- **Q88.** What do you do in the garden? Tag and hide and seek like candy land? Collect vegetables? Something new?
- **Q89.** Bigger than candy land: what makes it big? A longer carrot juice river? Hills? Fields? More to find?
