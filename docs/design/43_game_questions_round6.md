# Beaver game: questions, round 6

These questions are about playing with friends, the multiplayer game. Thank you for the first answers. There are no wrong
answers. Write short answers under each question. If you are not sure, write "not sure" and we can come back to it.

## Sleeping and skipping

- **Q91.** One player wants to sleep until the next stage, and the others are still playing. What should happen? (a) The game asks
    the others, and it skips when all say yes. (b) Only the host can skip. (c) Each beaver ages by itself while it sleeps, but the
    seasons and the pond stay in step with everyone. Which one do you like?
- **Q92.** Can one friend say no, and so stop the skip for the others? Or does the one who says no just stay awake while the
    others sleep, and then wake to find the day gone?

## Seeing friends

- **Q93.** How do you tell the friends apart? A name over the head? A colour of the fur, or a hat? A different tail?
- **Q94.** Can you talk to a friend in the game? A slap of the tail, a babble, or a thumbs up button? Or do you just talk in the
    room?

## Starting and joining

- **Q95.** One tablet says "Host", the others say "Join". Is that right? Who should be the host most of the time, you?
- **Q96.** A friend comes late to a game that is already running. Where should they start: next to you, or at their own lodge if
    they have one there?
- **Q97.** A friend plays at your place and builds a lodge in your valley. Next time they play at another friend's place. Where is
    their lodge? Stays in your valley (they come back to play it), or comes with them?

## Building and chopping

- **Q98.** A tree one friend cuts down is gone for everyone. Fine? Do trees grow back sooner when there are four of you?
- **Q99.** A friend builds a dam above your pond and your pond gets low. Is this part of the game, or should something help? (I
    made it easy to walk into a low lodge, so nobody gets stuck.)
- **Q100.** Helping: can a friend carry wood or mud to your dam and lodge and place it, so it counts for you? Do you get a thank you
    (a chime, a heart)?

## Families together

- **Q101.** Can two friends' beavers become partners and share a lodge, or is every family only its own? (This changes the saves, so
    "not yet" is a good answer.)
- **Q102.** One friend is a baby and one is an adult. Can the baby follow the adult around and share its food? Or do they just
    meet?

## More ideas

- **Q103.** Anything in a game with friends that you really want? A race, a trade of food, hide and seek? Write it down.
