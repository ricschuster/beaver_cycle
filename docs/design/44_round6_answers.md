# Beaver game: round 6 answers

Transcribed from the answered round 6 handout (`43_game_questions_round6.docx`, dated 2026-10-20, Q91 to Q103). Spelling is
tidied and the meaning is kept. The handout stays the source. The design that follows from them is in
[42_multiplayer.md](42_multiplayer.md), the follow-up questions in [45_game_questions_round7.md](45_game_questions_round7.md).

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 91 | Skipping with friends still playing | (a) The game asks the others, and it skips when all say yes. |
| 92 | Can one friend say no | Yes, it stops the skip. All have to agree on a skip. |
| 93 | Telling friends apart | You should choose how your beaver looks at the start of a game. This needs to be built. |
| 94 | Talking to a friend | Yes, talk to a friend in the game. |
| 95 | Host and Join | One is host, the others join. Anyone can be host. Like Stardew Valley: everyone needs the IP of the host. |
| 96 | A late friend | Starts at their own lodge. Maybe the game starts with 5 lodges close to each other, so a new player has a lodge to spawn into: up to 4 join, 5 in total. |
| 97 | A lodge when playing at another friend's place | Not understood. There is one game per save, and the main save is on the host's tablet. The location does not change. Could tablet and browser be combined? |
| 98 | Trees | Gone for everyone. They do not grow back sooner. |
| 99 | A dam above a friend's pond | "Does that actually happen?" If it happens, talk about it more generally. |
| 100 | Helping | A friend can carry wood or mud and place it. A thank you would be nice, a chime and a heart. |
| 101 | Partners across families | They can become partners. The mechanics are not clear yet, discuss more. |
| 102 | A baby and an adult | They can share food, but the baby does not follow. It stays with its family while it is a baby. |
| 103 | More ideas | Play in the dreams together. Games in the main game, hide and seek is a good one. Trade would be nice, mechanics not clear yet. |
