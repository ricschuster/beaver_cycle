## Five lodges

- **Q106.** The five lodges at the start: does each have a family in it (Mama and Papa, and a baby for the player to play)? Or are
    the extra lodges empty until a friend comes?
- **Q107.** If only two of you play, what lives in the other three lodges? Other beaver families like the ones in the game now? Or
    nobody?
- **Q108.** What happens in your lodge when you are not in the game? Does your family go on with its day, or does it sit still until
    you come back?

## Beavers that look different

- **Q109.** Choosing how your beaver looks at the start: what can you choose? The fur colour? A hat or scarf? The size of the tail?
    A name? Pick the ones you want most.

## Partners

- **Q110.** Two friends' beavers become partners, both adults. Whose lodge do they live in? Or do they build a new one together?
- **Q111.** When they have a kit, who plays it? Both friends take turns, or the kit goes to one friend and the other friend plays a
    parent who helps? Or each friend gets their own kit?
- **Q112.** Do the two friends have to say yes, like asking "do you want to be partners?" with a button?

## Playing together

- **Q113.** Dreaming together: do you all have to be asleep to dream together? Or can you walk into the same dream when you fall
    asleep, even at different times? Can some of you wake up while the others go on dreaming?
- **Q114.** Hide and seek in the main game: who hides, and who counts? Where do you hide, in the lodge, in a pond, behind trees?
    How do you win?
- **Q115.** Trading food: how do you give food to a friend? Put it on the ground? Hand it over with a button? Do you swap, or just
    give?
