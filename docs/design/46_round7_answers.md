# Beaver game: round 7 answers

Transcribed from the answered round 7 handout (`45_game_questions_round7.docx`, dated 2026-10-20, Q104 to Q117). Spelling is tidied
and the meaning is kept. The handout stays the source. The design is in [42_multiplayer.md](42_multiplayer.md).

| Q | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 104 | A friend in another friend's game | A new family with a new lodge. Games are independent of each other. |
| 105 | A laptop browser | Join as a player. |
| 106 | The five lodges at the start | Each has a family in it until a player starts. The player takes on the role of the baby or young one. |
| 107 | Lodges nobody plays | Other families, like the ones in the game now. |
| 108 | A family while its player is away | Goes on with its day, like an NPC family now. If life stages advance, they advance for the family too. |
| 109 | Choosing the look | Fur colour, hat and scarf, tail colour, and a name. |
| 110 | Partners' lodge | They build a new lodge together. |
| 111 | Kits of two players | Each gets their own kit. |
| 112 | Asking to be partners | Yes, with a button or in a dialog. |
| 113 | Dreaming together | Be asleep to dream together. A popup could show when beavers go to sleep. Maybe allow sleep outside a lodge, especially early as an adult, when building a dam and a lodge in one day is very hard. Not sure yet. |
| 114 | Hide and seek | Behind trees, with the same mechanics as in the dream. A note could go to friends who are not too far away, asking if they want to join. They walk to the beavers who want to play, then the game starts. |
| 115 | Trading food | Put it on the ground, which is easiest. Gifts are good too, only working when the other beaver has nothing in its mouth. |
| 116 | Talking | Not sure. Maybe the same kind of dialog as with other beavers. A player who is asked a question needs to be able to answer. |
| 117 | More ideas | None yet. |
