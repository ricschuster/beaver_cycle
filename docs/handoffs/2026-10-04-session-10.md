# Session Handoff: 2026-10-04 (session 10)

## Summary

M3 started, with the beaver itself. The shallow bog lake bed landed, and the
beaver now has a model and can swim, dive, and slap its tail. All PRs are
merged (#75, #77 to #80). The next session adds touch controls, then the
breath meter and the camera.

## Completed

- PR #75: the lake bed fades to shallow toward the south end (a ramp over
  z 1100 to 1700 m) and near OSM wetland cells (60 m reach). The bog is under
  0.5 m, open water keeps its depth. Constants are at the top of
  `tools/tiles/build_tiles.py` (`BOG_*`). The test menu readout now shows x, z,
  ground height, and water depth under the beaver.
- PR #77: `src/render/beaverModel.ts`, a simple beaver from three.js shapes. The
  tail and legs sit in their own groups. Legs swing and the body bobs while it
  moves.
- PR #78: walk or swim mode from the water depth (swim from 0.6 m, walk again
  below 0.4 m). Speeds from `09_game_length_and_speed.md`: 6 m/s walking (was a
  placeholder 2), 9 swimming. `BeaverState.y` is now the feet height, the water
  surface minus 0.2 m when swimming. The test menu jumps to the named markers
  (Almsee, the bog, the inflows) and scrolls.
- PR #79: dive mode. Hold Space in water at least 1 m deep to sink at 2 m/s
  toward the bed, release to rise. Dive speed 7 m/s. The model pitches its nose
  with the vertical movement. Fixed in passing: `main.ts` had dropped the dive
  flag.
- PR #80: tail slap. Press T for a 0.7 s slap, 1 s cooldown, on land or at the
  surface, not under water. The sim keeps `lastSlap` (time and place) for other
  beavers to hear later (Q26). A ring spreads over the water on a swimmer's slap.
  `Actions` has optional `dive` (held) and `slap` (press only).
- Issues: #76 parked the idea of a temporary on-screen stick (now covered by
  M3 touch controls). #54 and #57 each have a comment saying the walk they wait
  on has not happened.
