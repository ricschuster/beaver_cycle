## Current state

- Tablet (Tab A9+, 90 Hz): 2 m fine tiles hold 89.8 fps with rings of 150 to
  450 m, up to 1.6 million triangles. 1 m cells stutter (frames of 120 to 145 ms
  when a 205,000 triangle tile is built) and are not adopted.
- Fine tiles are 4.5 MB at 2 m. The first tile build downloads about 100 MB of
  the BEV 1 m terrain into `data/raw/` (about 3 minutes), then the build is
  about 30 s.
- The shore and bog look smooth in a headless screenshot. The author has not
  yet walked them on the tablet.

  beside lakes, not along streams or at the lodge site. The limits from session
  10 still hold (no breath limit, camera in tree trunks, Test button under the
  status bar, no touch controls).
- 75 unit tests and 5 tile tool tests pass. `npm run test:e2e` still was not run
  (port 5173 is held by another project's dev server).
- The leg animation runs at a fixed rate, so at 12 m/s the beaver may look slow
  footed. Nobody has judged it yet.
