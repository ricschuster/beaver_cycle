# Session Handoff: 2026-10-04 (session 13)

## Summary

A desktop session again. The camera now stays out of
trees and off the water surface, the sim's water edge matches the drawn
shoreline, and the tile tool now covers all six locations and the Totes
Gebirge marker, so no test menu button is greyed out. All PRs are merged.

## Completed

- PR #91: the breath meter from session 12, merged.
- PR #92: camera clearance. `src/render/cameraClear.ts` pulls the camera in
  before a tree cone (shortens at once, eases back out at 8 m/s) and keeps it
  out of a 0.4 m band around the lake surface. Under the surface the background
  and fog turn blue and short. `TreeGrid` in `src/terrain/trees.ts` answers
  "is this point inside a tree" from a 16 m bucket grid per tile, and
  `TREE_SHAPE` holds the cone sizes shared with the renderer.
- PR #93: `TileStore.waterDepth` uses the rule of the drawn lake mesh (a lake
  cell or one beside it, with ground below the lake level), and `isWater` is
  `waterDepth > 0`. The sim edge is now the visible shoreline.
- PR #94: `build_tiles.py` cuts a sparse tile set over the play area: the core
  rectangle as before, plus tiles within 640 m of the other locations and
  markers. About 570 tiles, 17 MB, 4 minutes. Anchors are enabled by tile
  presence. The e2e test checks the three former greyed buttons and that East
  and Hinterer Springbach stand on real ground. The lake bed pytests now look
  only at tiles of the Almsee level.

## Touch controls (added after the session)

- PR #96 (merged): `src/ui/touchControls.ts` draws a stick at the bottom left
  and a held Dive and a Slap button at the bottom right, clear of the breath
  bar. They feed the same `Actions` as the keyboard, merged by `mergeActions`
  in `src/actions/actions.ts`. Dragging the empty view still turns the camera,
  and a stick finger and a drag finger work together.
- They show on devices that report touch, or with `?touch=1`.
- `touchTuning` in `src/actions/stick.ts` holds the stick radius, dead zone,
  and button size. Closes #76; #36 stays open for its other items.
- Tried on the tablet (SM-X210) through the live shell: the author said it
  feels good. Not yet checked: frame rate with the larger world.
- The e2e test sends synthetic pointer events for the stick, since Playwright
  cannot drag with several fingers.
