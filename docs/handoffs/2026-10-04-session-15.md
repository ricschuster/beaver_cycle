# Session Handoff: 2026-10-04 (session 15)

## Summary

A session on the view distance. The author found the background fog heavy and
the distant peaks cut off. The cause was the data, not the fog: the tile tool
kept only 640 m of ground around the play area, so ridges beyond it simply
ended. The tiles now carry a far backdrop out to 5 km, the download box reaches
further south for the Totes Gebirge, and the fog and camera far plane were
raised to match. M2 (#35) was ticked and closed at the start. The PR is open.

## Completed

- Closed #35 (M2: Streamed core) after checking that all its task issues were
  closed.
- Far backdrop in `tools/tiles/build_tiles.py`: 2164 far tiles within 5 km of
  the play area and inside the BEV raster. A far tile has a level 2 file only,
  at 40 m cells (8 per side), and is marked `"far": true` in the index. `check()`
  tests that they meet their neighbours. A per tile block mode that was
  recomputed on the whole grid is now computed once. `--no-backdrop` turns it
  off.

  tile only (`src/terrain/streamer.ts`), with a unit test.

  9000 m (was 3600).
- `BBOX` in `tools/area-editor/build_data.py` now starts at 47.590 in the south
  (was 47.648). BEV terrain, OSM land cover and water were downloaded again.
  The old files are not kept.
- ADR 0006 has an addendum for the backdrop and the box.
