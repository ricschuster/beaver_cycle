## Current state

- The play area is Core 6.3, North extension 1.6, South extension 2.5, East
  extension to the Oedseen 2.4, Vorderer Springbach valley 1.1, and Hinterer
  Springbach valley 0.8 km2. The shapes are in `data/areas/areas.geojson`. All
  six are approved and switchable in the editor.
- Straight line across all six is 9.7 km, about 81 minutes at 2 m/s with no
  compression. Game length is parked, so this may change.
- Rebuild the editor data with `python3 tools/area-editor/build_data.py`
  (downloads and caches into `data/raw/`, untracked, about 50 MB). After
  changing a play shape, run `tools/area-editor/travel_graph.py` first. Open
  `tools/area-editor/index.html` in a browser.
- The round 1 answers settle: the beaver cannot die or lose, hurt beavers heal
  by eating lily pads, side quests are the beaver's dreams, stuck beavers are
  potential guides the player rescues, seasons are in, there are no coyotes, a
  baby takes a year to become a teenager who stays one more year and helps at
  the pond, and the family has a papa.
- Round 2 now has 38 questions (Q13 to Q19, Q23 to Q53).
- Known limits: the editor's crossing time is a straight line and ignores
  winding routes. The wetland class is almost empty in OSM, so the south bog is
  not mapped. Lake level in the terrain is about 587 m against 589 m published.
- The editor was tested in a headless DOM, not scripted in a real browser. The
  author has used it and approved the result. The test scripts are not in the
  repo.
