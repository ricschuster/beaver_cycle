## Current state

- Measured on the tablet (90 Hz screen, so fps is capped at 89.8): normal play
  is 0.5 to 0.75 million triangles and 27,000 to 44,000 trees at 89.8 fps. A
  ten minute soak was flat at 170 to 177 MB with the skin sensor at 29 C. The
  stress sweep held 90 fps to about 3 million triangles and fell to 60 fps at
  4.3 million. x100 outruns loading without hitches.
- Budgets (provisional, re-measure as detail is added): at most 1.5 million
  triangles, 100,000 trees, memory under 400 MB.
- Known limits: the camera can sit against a tree trunk (it only avoids the
  ground), the Test button sits under the status bar icons, the shallow lake
  bed and dark lake patch from session 7 remain, and the other location anchors
  are unchecked as landing spots.
- Pinch zoom and touch drag work in the e2e test with a mouse only. Touch on the
  tablet was not tried by hand.
