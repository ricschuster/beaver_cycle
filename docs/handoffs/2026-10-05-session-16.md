# Session Handoff: 2026-10-05 (session 16)

## Summary

A short session on tablet performance. M3 (#36) was checked against the code,
the tablet was set up again, and the bench and stress sweep were run with the
5 km far backdrop from session 15. The backdrop cost draw calls more than
triangles, so its tiles are now merged into chunk meshes. That is merged in PR #101.

## Completed

- Checked the M3 list in #36 against the code. All four items are built (third
  person beaver, camera at trees, ground, and water, breath meter, touch
  controls). The issue is still open, to close with a note on what needs tablet
  play (below).
- Ran `bench.mjs scenarios` and `stress` on the tablet with the far backdrop.
  Before the change: 82 to 88 fps, 1.3 to 1.9 million triangles in normal play
  (the backdrop is about 1.1 million of that), and a stress limit near 3.4
  million triangles and 200,000 trees before fps falls under 75.
- PR #101, merged (`perf/merge-far-tiles`): far tiles are merged into chunks of 8 x 8
  tiles in `src/render/terrainView.ts`. Core and walking went from 83 to 89.8
  fps (the screen cap), p99 from 22 to 11 ms, heap from 32 to 36 MB. The doc
  `docs/design/14_m2_streamed_core_plan.md` has the numbers, and its triangle
  budget is raised from 1.5 to 2.5 million.
