# Session Handoff: 2026-10-05 (session 21)

## Summary

Finished the visible part of M4: the world now changes with the seasons, and the
day is twice as long. The cascaded shadows were measured on the tablet for the
first time, and they cost about half the frame rate. The shadow default is left
as an open decision (#120). The light looks good on the tablet to the author.

## Completed

- #118: test menu buttons for time of day, season, lodge, sleep, and shadows
  (the open PR from session 20, merged).
- #119: seasonal ground and tree colours (`src/render/seasonLook.ts`). Spring is
  fresher, summer is the base look, autumn is ochre and brown, winter is snow on
  meadows and a pale forest floor with darker trees. Colours hold through the
  middle of a season and blend over three days at each turn. The ground is
  tinted in the shader by a per vertex weight (`seasonWeight`, grass and
  forest), so tiles are not rebuilt; rock and lake bed keep their colour. The
  hook is chained after CSM's `onBeforeCompile`.
- #121: shadow cost write-up in `docs/design/16_shadow_cost.md`, with the
  tablet numbers and four options. `bench.mjs` takes `BENCH_QUERY` to add URL
  options such as `cascades=1` to every load.
- #122: the game day doubled to 8 real minutes (`DAY_SECONDS = 480`), at the
  author's request: 5 minutes of light, 3 of night, an 80 minute season, a 5
  hour 20 minute year, and 16 minutes per day in the lodge.
- Issue #120: decide the shadow default and the tablet quality budget.

## Current state

- Tablet measurements (Tab A9+, 90 Hz), walking in core: two cascades 48 fps,
  one cascade 57, no shadows 90 (the cap). Lakes: almsee is the worst, 40 fps
  by default with 56 ms worst frames. `?shadowmap=1024` does not help, so the
  cost is geometry drawn into the maps, not map size. The seasonal tint adds no
  measurable cost.
- The default is still two cascades. Options for it are in `16_shadow_cost.md`:
  one cascade by default, less casting in the far cascade, a coarser ground copy
  for casting, or shadows off by default. Only the first is measured.
- Light by eye on the tablet: good at the values in `src/render/skyLook.ts`. The
  season colours in `seasonLook.ts` were seen in headless screenshots only for
  autumn and winter, and are a first guess.
- Seasons show in the ground, the trees, and the sky colour. Not yet: falling
  leaves, snow on branches, a seasonal lake, a moon or stars, and the underwater
  colour at night.
- The lodge is still only a test toggle until M5.
- The 8 minute day has not been played long. Whether a 3 minute night feels long
  is open.
- Sessions 13 and 14 limits and the stale water edge text in ADR 0008 (#111)
  still stand.
