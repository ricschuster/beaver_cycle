# Session Handoff: 2026-10-05 (session 22)

## Summary

M4 polish, started from the optional list. The day length is settled, and the sky
and trees now follow the night and the seasons more: a moon and stars at night, a
dark underwater view at night, and two kinds of tree, so that broadleaf trees turn
orange, drop leaves in autumn, and stand bare in winter. The shadow default (#120)
is still open.

## Completed

- Day length: the author played the 8 minute day and keeps it (#114 closed). The
  M4 plan and the session 21 next actions were updated (#126).
- Night sky (#127, `src/render/nightSky.ts`): a moon that rises in the east and
  sets in the west over the night, 700 seeded stars, both faded in by the daylight
  ramp, and a dark underwater colour at night (`skyLook`).
- Tree kinds (#128, `src/terrain/trees.ts`): each tree is a conifer or a broadleaf
  tree. The kind comes from the position (`treeKind`), so it is stable on every
  device, in 60 m stands: about three in ten stands are mostly broadleaf, about a
  quarter of all trees. The placement array now has five floats (x, y, z, scale,
  kind). Conifers are unchanged.
- Broadleaf look (#128): a low crown on a trunk with three thin branches, with its
  own seasonal colour in `seasonLook.ts` (`leaf`): fresh green, green, orange, bare.
  In winter the crown shrinks to nothing in the vertex shader (`foliage`, also in
  the shadow pass), over the three day blend, leaving trunk and branches.
- Autumn leaf fall (#128, `src/render/fallingLeaves.ts`): up to 140 leaves drift
  from broadleaf crowns within 45 m of the beaver, ramped by `leafFall` in the
  season look. Pure state, with tests.
