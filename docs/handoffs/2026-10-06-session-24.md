# Session Handoff: 2026-10-06 (session 24)

M5 ("Doing things", #38) started. Three small PRs landed the first slice: gnaw a

## Completed

- #134: chop. E (or the Gnaw button) near a tree, stand still 3 seconds, the tree
  is felled. The sim sees trees only through a `TreeSource` interface
  (`src/sim/trees.ts`); felled trees are stable ids from position (half a metre)
  in `WorldState.felled`, left out when a tile's trees are placed, and the tile
  is rebuilt.
- #135: the author's change, logs are dragged and not carried. A felled tree
  lies as a log (`src/sim/logs.ts`, 6 m from a big tree, 4 m from a small one).
  G (or the Drag button) grabs the nearest log in reach, one at a time, and
  another press or a dive lets go. Dragging slows the beaver to 60 percent. Logs
  float in water deeper than 0.4 m. `src/render/logView.ts` draws them. The first
  "carry wood, 4 logs" paws were removed.
- #136: the author's change, no walking through things. Trunks are solid and the
  beaver slides round them. On land it climbs over a lying log, in water it
  shoves floating logs aside, and a diver swims under. Moves are swept in 0.4 m
  steps so a fast beaver cannot tunnel.
- The M5 note in `13_milestone_plan.md` records all of this.

## Current state

- A small HUD (`src/ui/carryHud.ts`) shows trees felled, dragging, and gnaw
  progress. The tree count is what the 30 tree wood achievement will use; the
  achievement itself is not made.
- Only the near, detailed trees (level 0 and fine tiles) can be felled and are
  solid. Level 1 trees have a grid and are solid too, but are far away.
- Known gaps: logs do not stop each other, the dragged log passes through trees,
  floating logs do not drift with the stream, there is no climbing animation, and
  a tree id from a fine tile may not match the same spot on a coarser tile (the
  seeds differ), so a felled tree can reappear after the tile level changes far
  away. Not seen as a problem yet.
- Numbers are first guesses: gnaw 3 s, reach 3 m, grab reach 2.5 m, drag speed
  60 percent, float depth 0.4 m, trunk 0.45 m, beaver radius 0.5 m.
- No e2e test for chop and drag: the tiles are untracked, so CI runs on the flat
  plane with no trees.
- Sessions 13 and 14 limits, the stale water edge text in ADR 0008 (#111), and
  the open shadow default (#120) still stand.
