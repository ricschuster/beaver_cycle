## Completed

- #138: build a lodge from dragged logs. B (or the Build button) while dragging a
  log places it (`src/sim/lodge.ts`, `build` in `src/sim/step.ts`). The first
  log starts the lodge 3 m ahead of the beaver in shallow water (0.1 to 1.2 m)
  with deep water within 6 m for the underwater entrance, as a real bank lodge
  or den has. Each later log adds wood within 5 m: a 6 m log is 2 units, a 4 m
  log 1, a whole lodge 10. `src/render/lodgeView.ts` draws a dome that grows
  with the wood. Near a whole lodge, B without a log goes in or out (half speed
  clock, bedtime at night), and walking away leaves it. The test menu toggle
  stays. The HUD shows lodge progress.
- #139: a preview while dragging a log with no lodge yet. A ring on the spot ahead
  is green where a lodge can start and red where not, and the HUD says why
  (face the water, too deep, no deep water near). `checkSite` returns the reason.
- The M5 note in `13_milestone_plan.md` records both.
- Answered the author's question on where real beavers put lodges: Eurasian
  beavers mostly dig bank dens and pile a lodge at the water's edge or in shallows
  with an underwater entrance. Lodges in the middle of a pond are mostly a dammed
  pond habit, so that waits for the dam.
