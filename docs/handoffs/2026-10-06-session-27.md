# Session Handoff: 2026-10-06 (session 27)

## Summary

The lodge room is bigger and no longer shows the ground. It is now 6 m in radius
for a family of up to 7 beavers, and it is drawn in a scene of its own instead of
hanging 3000 m above the world. It passed typecheck, unit tests, build, and e2e
(without `public/tiles`, as in CI). A headless screenshot showed only the room.
It has not been tried on the tablet.

## Completed

- #146: the room is 6 m in radius and 3.5 m high, with a 1.2 m water hole at the
  centre and a nest for each of 7 beavers round the wall (`NEST_SPOTS`, `NEST` is
  the first). All 7 are drawn, and sleep works on any of them. The beaver appears
  3 m from the centre on the entrance side when it swims in (`ENTER_DISTANCE`).
- The room is drawn in its own `THREE.Scene` (`createLodgeInterior` in
  `src/render/lodgeInterior.ts`), which the renderer draws instead of the world
  while the beaver is in. The beaver model moves between the two scenes. The 3000 m
  lift (`INTERIOR_LIFT`) is gone. The camera is held under the ceiling and inside
  the dome's width at its height. The interior light was raised for the bigger
  room, then toned down to 55 after the nests looked too bright.
- ADR 0009 (`docs/decisions/0009-lodge-interior-own-scene.md`) records the choice.
  The M5 note in `13_milestone_plan.md` is updated.
- Decided with the author: the quick fix (hide the world, keep the lift) was
  offered, and the separate scene was chosen.
