## Completed

- #157 closed after session 32.
- #165: `src/sim/wear.ts`. Condition is a pure function of the game clock (a
  `Wear` record of `level` at time `since`), so sleep and season jumps age a
  building with no tick. The lodge is worn (0.5) after 40 days, the store after
  20. Both start new when the last log of the lodge goes on. Placing a dragged
  log with Build next to a worn piece mends it by 0.1 a wood unit (a long log
  is 2), the worse one first when both are in reach, and a good piece keeps the
  log. A broken piece (below 0.15) needs the full set: 10 wood units for the
  lodge, 4 for the store. Effects: a worn store holds 24 of 40, a broken one
  nothing (its contents stay), a broken lodge cannot be entered (someone inside
  stays). A gold pulsing ring (`src/render/repairGlow.ts`) marks a worn or broken
  piece, the lodge goes grey and slumps. `15_build_and_repair.md` has a "Built so
  far" section.
- #166: the HUD says "store worn 25%, needs 8 wood (a long log is 2)" near a
  worn piece, with a Build hint while dragging, and the store line shows its real
  capacity.
- #167: `?age=N` starts the lodge and store N game days old. `?age=45` gives a
  worn lodge and a broken store, `?age=70` a broken lodge. It combines with
  `?day=`.
