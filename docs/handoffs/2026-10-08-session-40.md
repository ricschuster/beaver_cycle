# Session Handoff: 2026-10-08 (session 40)

## Summary

The author found the test pond and the stream did not overlap. We measured why, agreed on a hybrid (OSM says
which streams exist, the terrain decides the exact line), added a line gate to the dam site scan, and snapped
the stream lines to the terrain channel in the tile build. The author checked it on the tablet: "Looks great!".

## Completed

- Measurements: the OSM lines lay a median 4 m from the lowest ground across them on the 5 m tiles (1 to 2 m on
  the 2 m fine tiles near the lake). At the first core spot only 3 percent of the pond lay within 3 m of the line.
- The line gate (#192, merged): the scan drops a site whose level 1 pond is not on the drawn stream (at least
  half the pond cells within 5 m of the line, and half the upstream line points inside it). `--no-line-gate`
  skips it. Sites carry `on_line` and `line_in_pond`.
- The snap (#193): `tools/tiles/snap_streams.py` moves each line, at most 15 m, to the lowest ground across it
  with a least cost path (Viterbi). Flat ground, lakes, and off-data points keep the OSM position, the ends
  stay, the heights are unchanged. `--no-snap` skips it. ADR 0010 and tests (9 new).
- Result at the dam sites: median offset 4 m to 1 m, share within 3 m 46 to 87 percent on the coarse tiles.
  First spot candidates 27 (gate only) to 48: core 13, others at least 7, east-oedseen 1.
- Decided: OSM licence stays a separate thread (ADR 0003). Lakes and land cover stay OSM either way.
