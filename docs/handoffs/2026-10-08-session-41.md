- #198, step 3, squash merged:
  - A dam gets a `wear` record when its last piece goes on and is worn after a season (10 days,
    `DAM_WORN_DAYS`).
  - The pond crest is the full 1.0 m while the condition is 0.5 or more, then falls with it, to nothing at 0
    (about 7 days of sag, then 3 days of drain). The crest moves in 5 cm steps, and `pondsAt` floods again only
    when a step changes, so the fill does not run every frame. `world.pondCrests` holds what the ponds were
    worked out at; `pondsOf` returns a `PondSet`.
  - The build button with a log, mud, or a rock mends a worn dam: 0.1 a wood unit, 0.1 mud, 0.2 a rock
    (a long log is 2 units). A broken dam is built again from nothing with the full set (8 wood, 4 mud, 2 rocks).
  - The repair mark (gold glow and HUD line) comes on below 0.5 and stays until the dam is back at 1.0
    (`Dam.repairing`, `damNeedsRepair`). A first version hid it at 0.5, so one rock looked like a full repair
    while 4 units were still missing (the author's catch).
  - A worn dam loses sticks and mounds in the view (down to a third), and glows gold.
  - Tests for dam wear, the pond follow, the HUD lines, `shownShare`. Doc 20 has a "step 3" section, doc 15 a
    line.
