# Session Handoff: 2026-10-08

## Summary

The author asked for a view of the map to fly around, not tied to the beaver or gameplay. We chose a free fly drone with tap to
fly to, built it as `?fly=free`, and tuned it to the author's taste (inverted look, wheel zoom, level W and S). The map view
`?fly=1` from earlier stays. Everything is in one pull request.

## Completed

- `?fly=free`: a drone camera (`src/render/flyCam.ts`, pure and unit tested). The stick or WASD flies it level over the ground,
  Space or E climbs, Shift or Q descends, a drag turns it (inverted both ways), the wheel flies in and out along the look, and a
  tap on the land glides to it and faces it (`pickGround`, `flyTarget`, `glide`). It keeps 3 m over the ground and the water and
  speeds up with height. Up and Down buttons on a tablet (`src/ui/flyButtons.ts`).
- `?fly=1` (map view): the wheel zooms, Q and E no longer do.
- `createCameraRig` got `onTap` and `onWheel` options, `ViewState` got `free`, and the renderer places the camera from it.
- An e2e test for keys, drag, and tap in the free view. The fly modes are described in `docs/design/20_dams_and_ponds.md`.
