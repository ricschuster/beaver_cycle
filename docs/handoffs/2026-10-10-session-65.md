## Next session: tune the manual

The manual was written from the code and the design notes, not from play, so it needs a read and a try. Suggested order:

1. Read `public/manual.html` in the game (press `?`) on the tablet and on a desktop, and note what reads wrong, too long, or too
   promising. Check the layout at tablet width and in landscape.
2. Follow the "first ten minutes" steps as a new player would, with `?welcome=1`, and fix any step that does not work as written
   (the white ring wording, mud scooping, the dam needing wood, mud, and rocks, where the log comes from).
3. Check the claims that were least verified: the partner and kit section (the lone beaver slaps and follows, moves in once the lodge
   is whole, a kit on the first day of spring), the "Dream" button (a placeholder), pinch to zoom, and what happens when breath runs out.
4. Decide whether the welcome card should say more, or whether the manual should open on a short "quick start" at the top.
5. Consider screenshots or small pictures of the rings and buttons, and a short controls card for the tablet.
6. The manual says nothing about saving, because no save or load was found in `main.ts`. Confirm and say so if a player would expect one.

## Risks or decisions needed

- The game starts as an adult beside a ready-made test lodge (the `?lodge=0` default). The manual says so, but the brief says you grow
  up in the lodge. Whether a real new game should start as a baby is a design call for the author.
- The manual is a second copy of the controls and rules. It can drift from `keyHints.ts`, the touch buttons, and the HUD text. CLAUDE.md
  now says to update it with them, but nothing checks it.
- The welcome card is skipped in automated browsers, so the playtests are not blocked, but it also means they never cover the first-run path
  except through `?welcome=1`.
- The still open items: rewrite #42, the `locations` playtest (ask before running it), animal variety in the south and the Springbach
  valleys, and the rest of the session 63 list.
