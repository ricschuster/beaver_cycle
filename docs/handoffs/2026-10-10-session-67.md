## Completed

- Read the session 66 handoff and reproduced the flake: `smoke.spec.ts` "the coach gives the first step" failed 4 of 6 runs with
  `public/tiles` moved away, and passed every time with the tiles.
- Found the cause with a temporary log in the `coachPending` block of `src/main.ts`. The first `requestAnimationFrame` timestamp
  can come before the `performance.now()` taken in setup, so `dt` went slightly negative and the clock fell from 24 s
  (`START_CLOCK`) to about 23.96 s. The adult beaver starts at exactly clock 24, so for that frame `lifeAt` said teenager, and the
  frame loop cleared `coachPending` without starting the coach. CI is slow to draw its first frame, which is why it showed there.
- PR #322 (`fix: never let the first frame step the clock back`): `dt` is clamped to 0 or more in `frame()`. With the tiles moved
  away the test then passed 6 of 6. Typecheck was clean. CI (`game` and `repo-hygiene`) passed on the PR, and it merged.
- Restarted the dev server on port 5199 when asked (it is a background process, not part of the repo).
