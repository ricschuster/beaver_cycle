# Session Handoff: 2026-10-10

## Summary

Session 73 profiled the long walking frames on the Tab A9+ (#110) and found the cause in the game's own code. It fixed the
stream search and the garbage collection, and turned the sun shadows off by default. Walking at x5 now has no frames over
35 ms in 12 s on most runs, at 84 to 90 fps.

## Completed

- Profiled with a Chrome trace and the V8 CPU profiler, then a heap sampling profile. The numbers and the story are in
  `docs/design/14_m2_streamed_core_plan.md` ("The profile of the long frames").
- PR #334 (merged): `streamDistance` was about 33 ms of each 50 to 57 ms frame. The rock view rebuild asks `wet()` about
  hundreds of rock spots, each scanning every stream segment of nine tiles. Streams now keep a bounding box and the call
  takes an `upTo` limit.
- PR #335 (merged): garbage collection. `Streamer.update` walked all 2733 tiles every frame building strings and objects.
  It now skips finished far tiles, keeps keys, lists nothing while fetch slots are full, and idles while nothing changed
  (`TileStore.version`). Far chunks merge in batches of 8. The ground colour table is built once. Allocation went from
  1.28 GB to 0.48 GB per 12 s, and about 174 MB with the shadows off.
- PR #336 (open when this was written, check it merged): shadows are off by default (`?shadows=1` turns them on), and a plain
  directional light follows the sun, because `?shadows=0` had also removed the sunlight (it only existed as the shadow
  system's lights). Manual and `docs/design/16_shadow_cost.md` updated. Closes the decision in #120 for now.
- `tools/tablet-live/profile.mjs` (`cpu` and `heap`) keeps the profiling recipe, described in the tablet-live README.
