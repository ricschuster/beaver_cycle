## Risks or decisions needed

- The talk takes Build only where `buildIsFree` says nothing else wants it. A new Build job must be added to it (`src/sim/talk.ts`).
- A wary family retreats only inside its pond; swimming into the middle of it may crowd them.
- Stuck beavers are found by `stuckNow` each frame (it caches the drift spot per family in a module Map). A change to how families
  are placed should keep the family id stable.
- New saved fields `rescued` and `guides` default to empty for old saves; `escort` is left out of the save. No `SAVE_VERSION` bump.
- A Playwright run leaves nothing to kill by hand, but `pkill -f playwright` in a Bash call kills its own shell. Do not use it.
- Decision for the author, carried from session 70: turn on `enforce_admins` so checks also bind admin merges?
- Older risks, still true: a save change that needs more than a default must bump `SAVE_VERSION` and add its step to `migrate`;
  Android WebView may clear `localStorage`; a New game replaces the save with no backup; `Streamer.update` idles unless
  `TileStore.version` changes.
