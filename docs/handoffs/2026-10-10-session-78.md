## Summary

The dream mini game (#131). The author pictured the beaver walking a little path in candy land and playing with marshmallow
beavers in a chocolate river, and answered two rounds of questions. I wrote the design note and a shared contract, three
sub-agents built the rules, the scene and the screen in parallel, and I joined them up. It is merged and installed on the
tablet as the standalone APK, but not yet played there.

## Completed

- **#374** (merged): design note `docs/design/29_dream_candy_land.md` with the author's answers (tag and hide and seek, sweets
  on the way, 3D, ends only on Wake up, sweet count for this dream only, a new land each night, a big group).
  - `src/sim/dream.ts`, `dreamLand.ts`: a seeded land (160 m square, chocolate river, path, 60 to 90 candy props, 40 to 60
    sweets, 7 marshmallow beavers, one per `MALLOW_COLOURS` coat), walk, swim, hop, splash, sugar rush, tag (60 s, no losing,
    shower of sweets) and hide and seek (count to ten, giggles, find all for a shower). 29 unit tests.
  - `src/render/dreamView.ts` and `dream*.ts`: its own WebGL canvas per dream, disposed on waking. `setColours` on the beaver
    model. Preview at `/tools/dream-preview/`.
  - `src/ui/dreamScreen.ts`: HUD, event lines, own touch controls (Hop, Splash, Play). `sleepPrompt.ts` hands over through
    `onDream` and `wakeFromDream`. `main.ts` stops stepping and drawing the world while dreaming. `?dream=1&seed=N` and a
    Dream now button in the test menu. Manual section "Dreaming in candy land".
  - `touchControls.ts` takes optional labels and an id prefix; a Space tap shorter than a frame now counts as a dive once.
- Played in headless Chromium with a one-off script: path and sweets, a full hide and seek, a tag round, Wake up to dawn with
  the clock untouched, no page errors.
