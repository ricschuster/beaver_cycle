# Session Handoff: 2026-10-12 (session 107)

## Summary

A short session on #174 (water features). The dark East-Oedseen water could not be reproduced, so it is closed as solved. The
Aagbach delta got a first funnel (#475, merged) and then a braided look (#476, open). Both were looked at in a desktop
render, not on the tablet.

## Completed

- East-Oedseen dark water: the Grosser Oedsee and the Almsee came out the same dark teal in spring morning light with the same
  camera, and no tile has lake cells without a lake level. Likely the double wound ribbon of #472, or the darker summer evening
  water colour. The author called it solved.
- #475 (merged): a stream ending in a lake flared to 2.2 times its width. It read as a funnel.
- #476 (open): replaces the flare with a braided delta. Where a stream ends within 1.5 m of its tile's lake level, up to three
  arms split off 60 m back and run straight to the nearest lake land cover within 120 m (at least 0.35 rad apart, half the
  width, none drawn if no water is found). At the Aagbach it gives four channels fanning into the two bays. Renderer only, no
  tile rebuild. Design note `docs/design/34_stream_width.md` has a section on it.
