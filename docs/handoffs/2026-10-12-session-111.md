## Completed

- #486 (merged): `make_lake_bed` in `tools/tiles/build_tiles.py` no longer drops the bed 0.4 to 0.8 m along the cell outline of a
  lake. It smooths the signed distance to the outline, tapers the bed to the lake level over 3 m, and lowers gentle land (under
  1 m above the level) within 8 m to meet it. The heights carry it, so `TileStore.waterDepth` and the render agree. A test for a
  flat shelf is in `tools/tiles/test_build_tiles.py`. Drone shots of the Alm outflow before and after show the steps gone.
- Audit of the tiles per location (nearest anchor). Fine 2 m tiles of the play tiles: core 18/82, north 24/103, south 11/193,
  east-oedseen 11/61, vorderer-springbach 6/59, hinterer-springbach 0/71. They exist only within 100 m of a lake. Stream density is
  0.6 to 1.4 km per km2 everywhere (south lowest). South is half forest, a third bare ground, 11% rock (OSM land cover). The game
  rules and content placement are the same everywhere; no content is core only.
- #487 filed: "Make all areas as detailed as the core", with the plan as a checklist.
- WIP branch `feat/fine-ground-near-streams` (commit pushed, no PR): `stream_near` adds ground within 100 m of a stream to
  `fine_tiles`. It gives 298 of 569 fine tiles in 4 regions (one of 30 x 33 tiles). Not built, not tested.
