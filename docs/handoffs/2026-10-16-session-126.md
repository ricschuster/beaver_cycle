# Session Handoff: 2026-10-16 (session 126)

## Summary

Read the session 125 handoff, started the dev server on 5174, closed #518, and took #458 (image textures) from a look to an
accepted decision and the first code. The author looked at `/tools/texture-preview/` and chose B, a small texture made in

## Completed

- #518 closed. The delta half was fixed in #521. The floating sky sheet could not be reproduced: 16 views from a free drone
  camera (8 m and 60 m up, four headings) at core (78, 1652) and (752, 1052) on the local tiles show far ridges fading into the
  fog and nothing floating. Probably fixed by far chunks showing only once whole (`updateFar`, `terrainView.ts`). Hinterer
  Springbach and the tablet were not checked.
- #458: PR #530 (merged) adds `tools/texture-preview/` (a boulder and a log with per face colour, a 64 px code made texture, and
  a 256 px image texture, triplanar, close up and far) and ADR 0012, accepted with the author's choice of B. Result: bark gains
  most close up, rock looks best with soft grain and not cracks, and from a few metres all three look alike. Memory and bundle
  cost are small (256 px is 0.33 MB on the GPU and about 40 KB as a PNG); fill rate on the tablet is not measured.
- Issues opened: #531 (shared grain material, bark on logs and sticks), #532 (rocks and mud), #533 (lodge, dam, food store),
  #534 (tablet check, and whether tree trunks get grain).
- #531: PR #535 (open). `src/render/grain.ts` has the bark texture and `grainMaterial`. `paintFaces` takes an optional grain per
  face, `stick` and the log trunk set it (bark sides 1, cut ends and the gnawed point 0) with a `uv` in repeats (`barkUv`), and
  `mergeParts` keeps `grain` and `uv` (parts without them get 0). Only `logView` uses it so far. Draw calls and triangles are
  unchanged (prop-preview: logs 3 meshes, 656 tris). 985 unit tests, typecheck and build pass. The e2e gate was not run.
