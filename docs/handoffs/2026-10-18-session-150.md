# Session Handoff: 2026-10-18 (session 150)

## Summary

The heat the author saw on a phone (#605) was found and fixed. The game drew a full frame on every screen refresh with no idle
mode, 116 fps on a 120 Hz Pixel 9 Pro. A 60 fps cap (#632) cut the game's power use by about half and the heating from 12.6 C to
0.9 C in ten minutes. #605 is closed with the numbers.

## Completed

- Measured on the desktop (GPU Chromium): about 990,000 triangles in 456 draw calls per frame, the same cost standing, walking and with
  Settings open, mostly three.js scene work on the CPU. `TerrainView.stats()` reports 2.5 million because it counts every loaded
  tile mesh, not only those in view.
- Measured on the phone over Wi-Fi adb, 10 minutes each: no cap (+12.6 C, light throttling, 105 mAh), the OS forced to 60 Hz
  (+3.4 C, 54.5 mAh), the game's own cap (+0.9 C, 44 mAh, fresh save). The author played it: motion feels fine.
- PR #632 (merged): `src/frameCap.ts` with 6 tests, wired into the frame loop in `src/main.ts`, dev only `?fps=N` (0 lifts it),
  and `docs/design/37_battery_and_heat.md` with all the numbers and the levers not built. #605 closed with a comment.
- The phone is back as found: screen timeout 2 minutes, refresh settings unset, adb on USB only, the test copy uninstalled.
