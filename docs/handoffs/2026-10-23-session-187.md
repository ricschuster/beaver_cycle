# Session Handoff: 2026-10-23 (session 187)

## Summary

Three small player-facing changes, each from the author's play of the tablets: the screen stays on while the game is played (#771),
eight save slots shown in two columns of four (#772), and a Back button on the look card of a new game (#775). All are merged and
the plain release build of `main` at `8f55986` is on both SM-X210 tablets. None of it has been tried on a tablet yet.

## Completed

- #773 (closes #771, #772): `src/ui/screenAwake.ts` holds a Screen Wake Lock from the start screen on, asks again when the page
  shows, lets go after 10 minutes without a touch or key (input takes it back). Settings has a "Screen stays on" On/Off choice
  (`keepAwake` in the prefs, default on). `SLOT_COUNT` 4 to 8, keys unchanged so saves stay in their slots. Manual (en, de), texts
  and `docs/design/26_saving.md` updated.
- #774 (follow-up for #772): the first PR left the slots in one column, which scrolled. The slot card is now wider and holds two
  columns of four from a 640 px window up (slots 1 to 4 left, 5 to 8 right); a phone keeps one column.
- #776 (closes #775): the look card of a new game, opened from the slot card, has Back next to Go. It returns to the slots with
  nothing written. A look picked on the way is not asked again (`lookPicked` in `src/main.ts`). The card of a new life stage and a
  guest's first look keep Go only. New e2e test in `tests/e2e/save.spec.ts`.
- Both tablets updated in place three times (saves backed up with `run-as tar` each time, `adb install -r`, saves kept). Both
  emulators were shut down at the end.
