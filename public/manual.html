Beaver Cycle: how to play

You are a beaver in a real valley in Austria. Nothing can hurt you and you cannot lose. There is no hurry. Look around and build something!

Your first day

You start as a baby beaver, at home in the lodge with Mama and Papa. The little card at the top left shows what to do. Its buttons are Back, Next and Close.

  1. Swim out. Walk to the water hole in the floor and press Dive (Space).
  2. Swim and dive. Hold Dive (Space) to go under water.
  3. Slap your tail. Press Slap (T) at the water surface.
  4. Go home and sleep. Dive in at the door, which is under water. Walk to the nest and pick Sleep until teenager.

Now you are a teenager, and you can gnaw, drag and build.

Your first dam

A teenager does these things. First have a snack in the lodge: take a lily pad, carry it in and press Eat (E) to eat it. Put another in the food store by the door. Then, in this order:

  1. Walk and swim

    Push the stick to walk. Drag the empty screen to turn the view. Walk into the water to swim.

  2. Gnaw a tree

    Walk up to a tree and stand still. Press Gnaw (E) until it falls. Gnaw is the Do button: the word on it changes with what you can do.

  3. Take the log

    The tree is now a log. Stand at the log and press Take (G) to hold it in your teeth. Press it again (it now says Drop) to let go.

  4. Carry it to a white ring

    Near a stream there is a white ring on the ground. That is a place for a dam. Carry the log into the ring.

  5. Press Build

    Stand in the ring and press Build (E). The dam also needs mud and rocks. Read the top left to see what is missing.

  6. Mud and rocks

    Stand in the shallows and press Take to scoop mud. Walk up to a rock and press Take to pick it up. Carry it into the ring and press Build.

  7. Watch the pond

    When the dam is whole, the water backs up into a pond. Lily pads grow there. Ponds are safe places.

You made a pond! When you are grown up, you will make a lodge of your own.

What do I do now?

Tap a question.

The top left has words. What are they?

They tell you what each button would do right now. For example: press Take to pick a lily pad. When you are not sure, read them.

Nothing happens when I press Take or Do
  • Stand still. Gnawing does not work while you walk.
  • Your mouth may be full. You can carry one thing. Press Drop (the Take button) to put it down.
  • Get closer to the tree, rock, lily pad or mud.
I cannot find a white ring

Walk along a stream. The rings show up when you are close. The little card at the top left says which way the nearest ring is.

The dam wants more

Read the top left. It lists the wood, mud and rocks that are still missing. Bring them one at a time and press Build in the ring.

I cannot build a lodge

Drag a log to shallow water. Face the water and press Build. The top left says what is wrong: too deep, no water in front, or no deep water nearby. Try another place.

I am a baby and cannot do anything

That is on purpose. Babies stay near Mama and Papa. Go inside the lodge and use the sleep buttons to grow up, or just explore near the pond.

I am lost

Look for the lake. The lodge sits at its edge. A grown-up can also use the Test menu to jump to a place.

The game is slow

Close the other apps on the tablet and open the game again. If it is still slow, ask a grown-up.

I closed the game. Is my beaver gone?

No. The game saves by itself every half minute. When you open it again, you see your games. Press Continue on yours. There are four places for games, so a friend can have one too. Rename gives a game a name. Delete throws a beaver away, so it asks twice.

Can I change how my beaver looks?

Yes, when a new game starts and every time a new baby is born. Pick a fur (five browns, each in five shades), a hat, a scarf, a tail colour and a name. The beaver turns above, so you can see it. Press Go when you like it.

The ? button at the top right opens this page. In here, Show the first steps brings the little card back.

The speaker button next to the ? turns the sound off and on. Near a stream you hear it rush. In the valley you hear the wind, birds by day, and crickets and an owl at night. It is quieter in the lodge and under water. What you do makes a sound too: gnawing, a tree falling, a splash, a tail slap, swimming, eating, picking things up and putting them down, and building: logs, mud and rocks on a dam or lodge, and a happy chime when a piece is done. Beavers babble when they talk, and a partner chirps when it comes with you. Soft music plays in the valley, and a brighter, tinkling tune in the dream. In the dream, sweets chime and the others giggle and cheer. Animals speak too. A duck quacks, and the owl, fox, lynx and bear each give a warning call when they notice you. Sound starts after your first touch.

The gear button opens Settings. You can make the music and the sounds louder or quieter, change the round map (turning, north up, or off), and turn the shadows on or off. Start screen saves your game and goes back to the list of games. Shadows look nice, but some tablets get slow with them, so they start off. When you change them the game starts again where you left off.

The cup button opens Things I did. It has four tabs: Building, Wood and food, Family, and Valley and animals. A tick means you did it. A ring means not yet. Things you count show a bar, like 3 of 10, and three dots for the three steps. Tap one to read how to win it above the tiles. Tap it again to hide the hint. With a mouse, just point at it.

New, to try: the round map under it shows the land around you. The yellow arrow is you. The brown dome is your home. N shows north. Up on the map is the way you look.

Dams and ponds

A dam needs wood, mud and rocks. A long log counts as two pieces of wood. You can carry one thing at a time.

When it is whole, you can go back and make it higher, up to three levels. A higher dam makes a bigger pond. A finished dam can open up the next dam spot nearby.

A green ring is a dam you have started. A gold glowing ring means something is worn and wants mending.

Your lodge

A lodge is made of ten pieces of wood. It sits in the water. Its door is under water: dive at the door to go in. When the pond is so low that you cannot dive, just walk in over the mud.

Inside you can eat, rest on the nest and sleep. To go out, stand at the water hole in the floor and press Dive.

Dams, the lodge and the food store wear out over time. A worn one shows gaps and a gold ring. Stand next to it, bring what the top left asks for and press Mend. If the lodge and the store both need wood, the top left names the worse one first and says the other needs wood too. If you wait too long it breaks, and you build it again. Nobody is hurt.

Food and the store
  • Lily pads: swim next to one and press Take. They die back in winter and grow back in spring.
  • Branches: press Gnaw at a fallen log to strip a branch.
  • Eating: carry the food into the lodge and press Eat. A lily pad makes you swim faster for a minute. A branch fills you up: you gnaw trees faster for a day.
  • The food store: when the lodge is whole, a food store sits in the water by the door. Swim to it and press Store. Tap a place to move food between the store and your mouth.

The store keeps food for winter. Nothing bad happens if it is empty.

Day, night and sleep

One game day is eight real minutes. There is a dawn, a bright day and a dark night. A season lasts ten days. The valley is green in spring, bright in summer, colourful in autumn and snowy in winter.

In the lodge a panel lets you rest:

  • Sleep until morning, at night.
  • Sleep until next season, on the nest.
  • Sleep until you grow up, for a baby or a teenager.
  • Dream, at night. See the next part.
Dreaming in candy land

At night in the lodge, press Dream. Your beaver falls asleep and dreams of candy land. It is a new land every night. In the dream your beaver is a marshmallow beaver too, in all the colours of the rainbow.

  • The path: follow the little path of candy stones. It leads past lollipop trees, cotton candy trees and gumdrop bushes down to a river of chocolate.
  • Sweets: walk or swim into a sweet to collect it. The count is at the top left. A golden star gives you a sugar rush, so you go extra fast for a moment. The sweets are only for this dream.
  • Golden beaver: somewhere off the path, a few steps away, stands a golden beaver with a piece of wood in its mouth. Touch it and you go super fast for a long time. It stands in a new place every night.
  • Chop and build: next to a lollipop tree or a cotton candy tree, press Play to chop it. You get a candy log in your mouth. Carry it to the chocolate river and press Play to start a candy dam, three logs make it whole. Away from the river, Play starts a candy lodge, four logs make it whole. When you press Wake up after building, you are asked if you want to keep them. If you do, they are there in your next dreams too, and you can add logs to them.
  • Marshmallow beavers: soft beavers in pink, mint, lemon and more. Each has a name. They come to say hello.
  • Tag: swim up to a marshmallow beaver in the chocolate river and press Play (E). Whoever is it chases the others. Touch someone to make them it. After a minute everyone cheers and sweets rain down.
  • Hide and seek: walk up to a marshmallow beaver on land and press Play (E). Then pick I seek (2) or I hide (1).
  • I seek: you count to ten while they hide. Listen for a giggle. Walk up to a hiding beaver to find it. Find them all for a shower of sweets.
  • I hide: they count to ten. Run and hide behind a big candy! Then they come looking. When you hear a giggle, one is near, so sneak round the other side. Being found is fun too. After a minute, or when they have all found you, sweets rain down.

The buttons are almost the same as in the valley. The stick or W A S D walks and swims. Hop (Space) hops on land and leaps out of the chocolate. Splash (T) splashes with your tail.

The dream lasts as long as you like. Press Wake up at the top right to wake. It is morning in the lodge.

Growing up and the next beaver

The game never ends. Each beaver grows up and then a kit takes over.

  1. Baby: stay near the lodge with Mama and Papa. Mama calls you back if you wander. You cannot fell trees or build yet.
  2. Teenager: you can do everything an adult can, and you help at the pond.
  3. Adult: Mama and Papa say it is time for a family of your own.

As an adult, look for a lone beaver. Listen for a tail slap far away, and follow the messages that say which way to go. After a message, soft gold sparkles on the ground point the way for a while. When you swim near it, it slaps its tail and follows you. Build a lodge and a dam in a good place. Your partner moves in when the lodge is whole. Now and then your partner tells you what it sees, such as a good dam spot or lily pads. On the first day of spring after a season together, a kit is born, and you play the kit.

Animals and other beavers
  • Deer and ducks are shy. Put a lily pad down near one and it will eat it and trust you more. A tail slap makes them jump.
  • Beaver families live in lodges on other ponds. At first they are shy and keep away. Put a branch or a lily pad down near them and they trust you more. When they trust you, the kits swim over to look. A tail slap scares them. Families talk about you, and news of a kind deed gets around: when you help one family, the others you know trust you a little more too. You can also hand food over: carry a branch or a lily pad up close to a beaver whose mouth is empty and press Give (the Do button). A shy family keeps away, so put a gift down for it first.
  • Talking: walk up to a beaver, such as Mama, Papa, your partner or a neighbour who trusts you. A line at the bottom says Willow is here, press Talk, for example. Press Talk (the Do button) and it answers. Even a baby can talk to Mama and Papa. You can talk to Mama and Papa inside the lodge too. Talk comes before Gnaw, so by the trees step a little away from the beaver to gnaw. The first time, a beaver tells you its name and its pond. Next time it knows you. Then you can tap a question. What are you doing? tells you what the beaver is busy with. Any news? gets you something true from a beaver who trusts you: where lily pads grow, that a fox is near, or that a dam needs mending. Can I help? gives you a job. A family may ask for a branch or a lily pad put down near them. Mama and Papa tell you what your home needs. You can ask two questions. Tap Bye and the beaver says goodbye. You may also hear a family call to each other.
  • Stuck in the snow: in winter you may hear someone calling for help. A beaver from a family that knows you is stuck in a snowdrift by its pond. Stand next to the drift, press Dig, and keep still until it is dug out. Then talk to it and tap Show me the way. It leads you to a lone beaver, a free dam spot, or home, and waits if you fall behind.
  • Otters and kingfishers lead you to lily pads. Follow them. They wait if you fall behind.
  • Foxes and owls may come for your food or chase you. Wolves come in pairs, and howl before they run at you. Only water is safe from a wolf. Slap your tail to scare them away. They can only frighten you, never hurt you. A pond is a safe place.
  • Other beaver families live in ponds around the valley.
Playing together

Up to four friends can play in one valley on the same Wi-Fi. One tablet is the host, the others join. It works with the app on a tablet, not in the Play Store version.

To host: on the start screen, pick the game, then tick Let friends join this game and start it. A message shows the address, like 192.168.0.7.

To join: on the start screen, type the host's address and press Join. Pick how your beaver looks, and you appear next to the lodge of a family that is free. You keep your own look.

Friends see each other, gnaw trees (a tree one friend fells is gone for all), drag logs, carry mud and rocks, and build and mend dams together. Stand close to a friend and press Talk (the Do button). A card opens with a few questions, such as Shall we swim together?. Tap one and your friend sees it with Yes! and Not now, and you see the answer. Hide and seek: tap Hide and seek? on the card and choose I hide or I seek. Every friend within 60 steps gets the question, and a friend who says Yes! walks over to you. A Yes! to Want to play? starts a game too. The seekers stand still and count to ten, and the hiders run off and hide behind a tree. A seeker finds a hider that it gets close to with no tree between them, and the hiders you have not found stay out of your sight. Nobody wins or loses. Two grown-up friends who stand close can also become partners: one taps Be partners? on the card, the other sees the question and taps Yes, partners!. Partners build a lodge together: either one can put the first log and either can finish or mend it. Swim in at its door, as at your own, and walk round the room. Press Dive at the water hole to swim out. Carry a lily pad or a branch in your mouth into the round room and press Eat to eat it. At the host's food store, press Store to put the piece in your mouth into the first free place. With an empty mouth, the same button takes the first piece out. On the first day of spring after the lodge is whole, each partner gets a kit and plays it. The two old beavers are Mama and Papa. To stop being partners, stand close and one taps Stop being partners? on the card, and the other taps Yes, stop. The lodge is then a home you left. The game is saved on the host's tablet only. When the host closes the game, everyone goes back to the start screen.

Achievements

Each life has its own list. A message shows when you win one. Things you count have three steps: a dot for each step you won, like ●○○, and a number for how far you are to the next one.

  • Wood: fell 30 trees. Then 100, then 300.
  • Home: build a lodge of your own.
  • First dam: finish a dam so the stream holds water.
  • Higher dam: make a dam as high as the spot allows.
  • Pioneer: build a dam at a spot that a pond opened up.
  • Pantry: fill the food store.
  • Water gardener: pick 20 lily pads. Then 60, then 150.
  • Mending: mend something worn until it is as good as new (Handy beaver). Then mend 10 (Fixer-upper), then 30 (Master mender).
  • Hauler: put 30 loads of mud and rock on dams. Then 100, then 300.
  • A full year: live through all four seasons.
  • Mate: bring a partner home to live in your lodge.
  • Kits: have a kit of your own. Then you play the kit.
  • Rescuing: dig a stuck beaver out of the snow. It becomes your guide (Guide). Then dig out 5 (Rescuer), then 15 (Snow rescuer).
  • Growing up: grow from a baby into a teenager.
  • Visit home: after you leave home, swim back and talk to Mama or Papa.
  • Hand down: have a kit while a dam you built is whole. Your kit gets your lodge and your dam.
  • Welcome: scoop mud, stand on the bank by your lodge and press Mark (E) to pat a scent mound. Next morning a beaver comes to sniff it. Walk up and say hello, and press Talk. After three days it walks off. If you said hello, it moves into a lodge on a pond nearby. The mound dries and is gone after four days.
  • Good neighbour: talk to beavers of 3 different families. Then 6, then 10.
  • Long line: play the kit of the kit of the kit of the first beaver. Then the 5th life, then the 10th.
  • Diver: dive and stay under water until you must come up for air.
  • Explorer: visit all six places of the valley.
  • Tail slapper: slap your tail 10 times. Then 50, then 200.
  • Well fed: eat 25 pieces of food. Then 100, then 300.
  • Stripper: strip 50 branches off logs. Then 150, then 500.
  • Winter store: have the food store at least half full on the first day of winter.
  • Many dams: have 3 whole dams standing at once. Then 5, then 8.
  • Big pond: make a big pond with one of your dams.
  • Animal friend: put down food for a deer, and for a duck. They eat it.
  • Pond life: make a pond that is old enough for ducks and frogs, and go and see both.
  • Homes far and wide: have a lodge or a whole dam of yours in each of the six places of the valley.
  • Sweet dreams: sleep at night and have a dream.
  • Long winter: live through a whole winter in your lodge and never let the food store run empty.
For grown-ups
  • The Test menu at the top right is for the author: it speeds up time, jumps to the six places in the valley, and, when the game is opened with ?shadows=1, turns the sun's shadows on or off. They are off by default because the tablet is too slow with them.
  • The Test menu has two volume sliders (effects and music, kept on the tablet) and a switch for each sound layer and one each for the music and the one-shot sounds. ?sound=0 starts the game without sound.
  • The Test menu's Dream now button opens a dream at any time. The address ?dream=1 starts the game in a dream, and ?seed=5 picks which candy land.
  • The game saves by itself (four games per tablet, each with an optional name) and shows them at the start. If the tablet clears the app's data, the save goes with it.
  • The game starts as a baby in a ready-made lodge. The coach card follows each life stage and starts again when the beaver grows up. Close turns it off. The Test menu can jump to a teenager or an adult.
  • If a dive runs out of breath, the beaver floats up. Nothing is lost.
  • The game is a work in progress. Tell the author what confused you. That is how this page gets better.
