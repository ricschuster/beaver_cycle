Beaver Cycle privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026. This applies to the game as a web page and as an Android app (com.beavercycle.game).

In short

Beaver Cycle collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. It does not send your play to anyone.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your saved game (one slot), your sound volumes, and whether you have seen the welcome card and the coach.
  • This stays on your device, in the browser storage of the page or the app. The author cannot see it.
  • Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

What the game loads from the internet

  • The web page loads the game, its sounds and the valley map tiles from its own site (GitHub Pages). The Android app carries them inside the app and needs no network to play.
  • Like any web server, the host of the page (GitHub) may log your address and browser when the page loads. That is GitHub's own logging, covered by its privacy statement. The author does not receive it.
  • The only link to another site is the map credit at the bottom of the screen, which opens openstreetmap.org when you tap it.

What the game does not use

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or advertising ID. No personal information is asked for, and no one under any age is asked to give any.

Children

The game is made to be played by children with a grown-up nearby. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing about a child to collect, share or delete.

Changes

If the game ever collects anything, for example for playing with others over a network, this page will change first and the date above will move.

Contact

Questions: write to ric.schuster@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/ricschuster/beaver_cycle/issues. The source code is at github.com/ricschuster/beaver_cycle.
